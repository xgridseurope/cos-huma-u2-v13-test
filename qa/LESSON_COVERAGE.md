## Textbook items p. 23–29 (111 items, excluding page-structure items)

- **Covered**: every concept, function, classification, process and fact of pp. 24–29, all 21 bone labels of p. 25 and all 15 muscle labels of p. 27 (shown as 3D labels; several are also tap targets).
- Book sentences are shown in a 📘 box with the page number, word for word. Boxes marked "📘 · resum" join several lines or bullets of the same page into one sentence (wording kept, only joined).
- Not covered (belong to pp. 30–37, outside this lesson): vocabulary "radiografia", "exercici"; Recordem 3 (posture when sitting).
- L3 extensions (irregular bones, girdles) deliberately left out.

## Content not in the book (always tagged in the UI)

- 💡 Curiositat · no surt al llibre: femur is the longest bone; Achilles tendon name; muscles can pull but not push.
- Reflex step 4 ("then the brain notices the cup is hot"): "Explicació addicional (no surt al llibre)".
- Examples in the voluntary/reflex sorting game (football, writing, blinking…) are everyday examples, not book examples.
- Humour lines and rhymes by Ossi ("Si el braç vull doblegar, el bíceps s'ha d'inflar!") are memory aids, not book text.

## Model limits shown to the child

- Ligaments are not in the model (stated in the joints step).
- Biceps/triceps thickness change is a schematic (stated in the biceps step).
- The full musculature is static; it is hidden automatically when the elbow bends (biceps/triceps stay and bend).
